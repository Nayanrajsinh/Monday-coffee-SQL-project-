📊 Monday Coffee Expansion — SQL Analytics Project

☕ Overview

Monday Coffee is an online coffee retailer operating across major Indian cities since January 2023. This project analyzes the company’s sales, customer behavior, and market potential using SQL. The final goal is to recommend the top three Indian cities for opening new physical coffee shops based on data-driven insights.

🎯 Project Objectives

Understand coffee consumption patterns across cities

Analyze sales performance and product demand

Segment customers and evaluate city-wise profitability

Identify high‑potential markets for expansion

Provide actionable insights using SQL queries and structured analysis

🗂 Dataset Description

The project uses multiple tables:

Table

Description

customers

Customer details including city

products

Coffee product catalog

sales

Transaction-level sales data

city_population

Population data for each city

city_rent

Average rent estimates per customer/city

🔍 Key SQL Questions & Insights

1. Coffee Consumers Count

Estimate coffee consumers per city assuming 25% of the population consumes coffee.

2. Total Revenue (Q4 2023)

Calculate total coffee sales revenue from Oct–Dec 2023.

3. Product Sales Count

Find total units sold for each coffee product.

4. Average Sales Amount per City

Compute average sales per customer for each city.

5. City Population & Coffee Consumers

List cities with population and estimated coffee-consuming population.

6. Top 3 Selling Products by City

Rank products by sales volume for each city.

7. Customer Segmentation

Count unique customers purchasing coffee products in each city.

8. Average Sale vs Rent

Compare average sale per customer with average rent per customer for each city.

9. Monthly Sales Growth

Calculate month‑over‑month sales growth percentage.

10. Market Potential Analysis

Identify top 3 cities based on:

Highest total sales

Total rent

Total customers

Estimated coffee consumers

🛠 Tech Stack

SQL (MySQL / PostgreSQL / SQL Server)

📝 Final Deliverables

Complete SQL query set

City-wise insights

Product performance analysis

Market potential ranking

Expansion recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project outputs the top 3 cities with the strongest market potential for opening new Monday Coffee shops.
